Research interests

I work in integrable probability and mathematical physics. My research focuses on interacting particle systems, percolation models, and vertex models within the Kardar–Parisi–Zhang universality class, particularly their long-time asymptotic behaviours and large-deviation phenomena. I am also interested in random matrix theory and related models from statistical mechanics.
